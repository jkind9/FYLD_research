# FYLD public product context

**Take-away:** This source helps identify potential users and decisions. It does not validate a reconstruction method.

## Source

[FYLD official website](https://fyld.ai/), accessed 1 October 2026. This is a company publication, not a peer-reviewed technical paper.

## What the source establishes

FYLD describes field operations in sectors including utilities, energy and heavy civil work. Its public material addresses field workers and managers and discusses visibility into field activity. This gives context for asking how a site capture might support remote understanding.

The website does not establish the accuracy, runtime or deployment readiness of the research prototype in this repository. It also does not establish access to private FYLD datasets, interfaces or product architecture.

## How we would use it

The proposed research question is whether a short capture can help a remote colleague understand an observed work area. That question needs a specific task: locating an exposed asset, inspecting visible progress or deciding whether another photograph is needed. These are candidate research uses, not claims about existing FYLD features.

A useful evaluation should compare the proposed map with the current information available to the manager. Measure whether the decision improves, how long it takes and how often missing evidence is recognized. A visually appealing map is insufficient if the decision stays unchanged or hidden surfaces appear observed.

Phone capture should fit the worker's existing safe workflow. The method should record capture time and requests for repeat footage. Offline capture and delayed upload are questions to test rather than assumptions about the product.

## Limits and permissions

Treat product claims as first-party context. No independent effectiveness result is inferred from them. Website access does not authorize copying private customer media, accessing internal systems or reusing trademarks as an endorsement.
