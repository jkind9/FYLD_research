# Method comparison

This table compares what each route requires. It is an engineering assessment based on the linked primary sources, not a cross-paper benchmark ranking. Local colour/depth and sparse image-only experiments are preliminary. Final implementation validation remains pending.

| Route | Required input | Scale and pose | Outputs | Local status | Main unanswered question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open3D colour/depth baseline | RGB, aligned metric depth, intrinsics and timestamps | Depth supplies metres; estimate poses or use reference poses for an oracle comparison | Trajectory and fused points; mesh integration is a later stage | Executed on a short TUM subset | Does geometry remain stable across longer and harder scenes? |
| [RTAB-Map](https://github.com/introlab/rtabmap) | Supported calibrated colour/depth or stereo sensor stream | Sensor scale with tracking and loop closure | Trajectory and map | Pinned source only | Can a permitted build improve revisits and recovery on the same data? |
| [COLMAP](https://colmap.github.io/) | Overlapping still images or selected video frames | Image reconstruction needs an independent scale anchor | Cameras, sparse/dense reconstruction | Preliminary sparse CPU run through pycolmap; no dense run | How much capture time and desktop processing are needed? |
| [MobiDepth](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/mobidepth-real-time-depth-estimation-using-on-device-dual-cameras/), [HiMoDepth](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10178038/), [HyperSight](https://lion.sjtu.edu.cn/publication/publicationDetail?id=64) | Accessible dual phone cameras, calibration and synchronization; HyperSight also uses motion | Calibrated stereo can supply scale; tracking still needed | Depth or ranging | Literature only | Does the chosen handset expose the required simultaneous cameras? |
| [MobileStereoNet](https://github.com/cogsys-tuebingen/mobilestereonet), [BANet](https://github.com/gangweix/BANet) | Rectified stereo plus permitted weights | Depth conversion needs known focal length and baseline | Disparity/depth per pair | Not executed | Do weights and datasets permit company use, and does hardware meet latency needs? |
| [ARCore Raw Depth](https://codelabs.developers.google.com/codelabs/arcore-rawdepthapi) | Supported Android device and recorded depth/confidence/camera metadata | Native metric depth and session pose; export conventions must be checked | Sparse depth and pose for fusion | Not captured | Are range, coverage, timing and thermal behaviour adequate outdoors? |
| [RoomPlan](https://developer.apple.com/augmented-reality/roomplan/) | Supported Apple device with LiDAR | Native room capture | Parametric indoor room model | Not captured | Does the target scene fit the indoor room assumptions? |
| [MASt3R-SLAM](https://github.com/rmurai0610/MASt3R-SLAM) | Images, permitted pretrained weights, CUDA setup | Learned scale must be checked against independent dimensions | Dense reconstruction and trajectory | Licence-blocked for company execution | Can permission be established, then performance reproduced? |
| [VGGT](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt), [VGGT-Ω](https://vggt-omega.github.io/), [InfiniteVGGT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02281) | Images and separately reviewed code/weights | Predicted geometry requires independent scale and drift checks | Cameras and scene geometry; streaming support varies | Current research leads only | Which version offers measured benefit within permitted hardware and terms? |

Measure identical frame subsets, calibration and depth validity rules for fair colour/depth comparisons. Record tracking failures, trajectory error, relative drift, wall time and memory. Reference camera poses form an oracle for isolating fusion error; they are not a competing tracker.

For area, define the measured surface first: a horizontal projection and an irregular 3D surface have different areas. Report observed coverage and unobserved regions. Validate dimensions and area against independent measurements before using construction language such as usable area or clearance.

Do not equate stereo end-point disparity error with depth error, trajectory error or area error. Do not compare frames per second from a phone paper to total desktop processing time without resolution, hardware and included stages.
